The display of corruption, ineptitude, self absorption, braggadocio, greed, deceit and most of all doublespeak from our president and his sycophantic underlings in the executive branch entertains us on an hourly basis. We're living through an ever accelerating tragifarce that rivals Netflix's movie offerings but is doing far greater harm than the brain rot induced by a Netflix subscription.

How many times can Trump claim a peace resolution with Iran is imminent while shorting the stock market just before the next Iranian lego satire airs on YouTube? It was recently reported that Trump's brokerage account made ["...3,642 individual trades made through the account in the first three months of 2026"][trump_trades]. And you guessed it, apparently the U.S. president is immune from any conflict of interest legal ramifications for personally trading stocks while in office. Past presidents at least set up blind trusts to manage their personal assets. With Trump there's nothing to hide. Remember when he bragged that he could [stand in the middle of 5th avenue and shoot someone][5th_avenue] without losing any voters? That was in 2016. Apparently today Trump believes that he can bomb Iran into the Stone Age for no good reason and still prevail in the midterms. Full disclosure, I'm no apologist for the Democrats who consistently line up en masse to approve U.S. defense budgets and approve supplying arms to Israel so it can murder its neighbors.

In the face of increasing U.S. attacks on Iran in the last week, you guessed it, Trump today claimed that Iran truce talks are moving at a rapid pace. The news coming out of Tasnim, the Iranian news agency was much different. The Iranians are suspending all dialogue with the U.S. In addition, the Iranians are threatening to respond forcefully to U.S. attacks and Israel's actions in Lebanon and Gaza. It seems that we're on the brink of all out war again.[^1] [^2]

Trump's peace maker delusions / shakedown artist aspirations aside, the continued choking off of Gulf petroleum products will have increasingly dire impacts on the world economy. And the U.S. won't be immune despite Trump's claims to the contrary. Fun fact. Oil companies stand to benefit hugely from high prices. Average Americans will be sucker punched once again since almost everything we rely upon is tied in one way or another to oil. Even high powered oil executives are [sounding the alarm][exxon_alarm]. With 20% of the world's oil off the market, once countries have exhausted strategic reserves we could see oil prices blasting further skyward.

We have a president whose modus operandi is to play the victim. Unfortunately, Trump lives in a Ptolemaic world of his own where the entire world, including Americans, revolve around his tiny planet. And he just happens to have more or less unchecked power to victimize all of us in his orbit. Let the drama continue.
