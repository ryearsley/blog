## Trump Sues Himself

In January 2026 Trump brought a personal lawsuit against the IRS because a contractor publicly leaked Trump's personal income tax returns. Right away that should raise a bunch of red flags. The president sues an agency over which he is the the executive? Okay, even if leaking Trump's tax returns was illegal or just inappropriate, what does Trump have to hide? Every modern president since Jimmy Carter on has released his tax returns voluntarily. In fact, going back to his first presidential campaign, Trump [promised to do the same][trump_taxes]. Of course a Trump promise is like a bad check that bounces when you take it to the bank.

The problem Trump faces is that it is well known that Trump's business practices over the years have been both shady and in many cases unsuccessful. Nevertheless, despite his ultimate refusal to voluntarily release his taxes, the New York Times published an exhaustive investigation into the matter in 2020. That investigation found that Trump paid only $750 in taxes in 2016 and 2017 and zero taxes in ten of the prior fifteen years. It also documented extensive business losses claimed by Trump, in addition to an ongoing IRS audit over a $72.9 million dollar refund Trump received in 2010. Yet Trump claims a net worth in the billions. Interesting? Corrupt? According to Trump himself, minimizing his taxes, "...makes me smart.". 

It recently came out that Trump has withdrawn his $10 billion dollar suit against the IRS after working out a special with with the Justice Department. Essentially this can be read as settling a lawsuit that Trump brought against himself, i.e. against the government he sits atop. But if you dig into the details it gets worse. On May 19 Todd Blanche, acting attorney general of the United States, announced that the Justice Department would create a [$1.8 billion dollar fund][doj_fund], a so called "Anti-Weaponization Fund", to be used to compensate Trump allies "unfairly" targeted by the Justice Department. The fund will be administered by a five member panel handpicked by Todd Blanche, including one member chosen in "consultation" with congressional leaders. Potentially this means that pardoned participants in the January 6 assault on the U.S. Capitol could apply and be compensated. The obvious corruption and misuse of power is both brazen and stunning.

A slush fund that hands taxpayer money to Trump cronies is bad enough but the story gets even slimier. The other [important outcome][irs_audit] of this spurious lawsuit is that the federal government agrees to cease any pending tax audits of Trump, his businesses and his family members. Furthermore, the agreement bars the government from investigating any past tax returns. The average American should be so lucky to have all past income taxes immune from audit.
